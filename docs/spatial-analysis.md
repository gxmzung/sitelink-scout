# Spatial Analysis — Architectural Space & Wireless Propagation

**Architectural research and design direction:** 박하음  
**Engineering and instrumentation:** 이영준

## 1. Research question
How do architectural layouts, reinforced-concrete elements, lightweight partitions, movable materials and occupancy conditions correlate with measured 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi RSSI?

The platform does **not** currently identify a material's intrinsic RF attenuation from an RSSI reading.

## 2. Architectural variables
| Feature | Record | Confounders |
|---|---|---|
| Reinforced concrete | geometry, thickness, reinforcement if known | reflection, reinforcement, angle |
| Lightweight partition | construction, layers, thickness | studs, openings, moisture |
| Glass | type, coating, thickness | metallic films, angle |
| Metal/equipment | position, extent | strong reflection/multipath |
| Temporary materials | type, stack position | movement and changing geometry |
| Occupancy | count, floor area, placement | posture, movement, body orientation |

All categories are hypotheses and scenario metadata, not calibrated loss coefficients.

## 3. Spatial scenarios
- **S01 Open baseline:** fixed AP and receiver locations, no added obstacle.
- **S02 Partition change:** same points, before/after movable partition.
- **S03 Structural barrier:** compare locations with and without intervening walls; avoid attributing every difference to one wall.
- **S04 Occupancy change:** repeated empty/occupied measurements at controlled density and distribution.
- **S05 Material arrangement:** relocate documented material stacks while controlling other conditions.

## 4. Simplified research model
```text
PL_est(d) = PL(d0) + 10 n log10(d/d0) + Σ L_obstacle + L_occupancy
```
`n` and added loss terms must be fitted to controlled observations. The equation is a conceptual log-distance model, **not a validated deterministic propagation solver**. Interactions, reflections, diffraction and multipath can violate additive assumptions.

For paired samples at fixed positions:
```text
RSSI_drop_dB = median(RSSI_baseline_dBm) - median(RSSI_scenario_dBm)
```
A positive drop describes an observed difference, not automatically a material-specific causal effect.

## 5. Proposed spatial dataset
```text
floor_id, floorplan_version, coordinate_system, scale_m_per_unit,
point_id, x_m, y_m, z_m, ap_id, bssid, scenario_id,
material_type, thickness_mm, occupancy_count, occupancy_area_m2,
source_type, measured_at, received_at, rssi_dbm
```
Proposed occupancy density: `occupancy_count / occupancy_area_m2`. Spatial coordinates, source type and scenario identifiers are **not guaranteed by the current measurement API**.

## 6. Architectural deliverables
Annotated floor plans, wall/material legend, measurement-grid diagrams, before/after layouts, occupancy scenarios, observed-point maps and clearly labeled estimated layers. Document authorship and design decisions with dated artifacts.

## 7. Limitations
Classic ESP32 measures 2.4 GHz only. RSSI varies with device, antenna orientation, AP power, multipath, traffic and time. A heatmap generated by interpolation is an **estimate between observations**, not a direct measurement.
